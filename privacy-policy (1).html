Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ZION OS ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information for our operating businesses, including Zion Hospitality and ZION Construction. This policy applies to all information collected through our booking process, project intake process, website, and SMS text messaging program.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information from guests, clients, and subcontractors:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Booking or project details relevant to the services you've engaged us for
  • Communications you send us, including text messages, emails, and form submissions

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the services you've requested (guest support, project updates, scheduling)
  • Respond to your questions and communications
  • Send you text message updates related to your reservation, project, or assignment, if you have opted in to receive them
SMS Consent and Mobile Information: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors and third parties is limited to what is required to deliver the services you've requested (for example, coordinating a project assignment) and does not include your mobile opt-in consent status or phone number for the purposes of SMS marketing.

Message Frequency and Cost

Message frequency varies depending on your reservation, project status, or assignment activity. You may receive anywhere from a few messages per project or stay up to several messages per week during active periods. Message and data rates may apply. Contact your mobile carrier for details on your plan.

Opting Out

You can opt out of receiving text messages from us at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one final confirmation message. To resume receiving messages, text START. For help, reply HELP or contact us using the information below.

Data Retention and Security

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the information below.
